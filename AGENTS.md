# Permanent System Instructions and Design Constraints

These constraints must be strictly adhered to for KORUS Real Estate and all future iterations or modifications of the codebase.

## Constraint 1: No Hero Subheading Tagline
- **Rule**: Never place a subheading, tagline, or pre-headline/eyebrow above the main H1 editorial title on any Hero or landing section.
- **Placement**: All Hero sections must begin immediately with the main H1 tag.

## Constraint 2: Aggressive Mobile Typography Scaling
- **Rule**: Significantly reduce font sizes on mobile breakpoints (max-width: 768px).
- **Target Desktop Title**: 64px – 80px
- **Target Mobile Title**: Must scale down to 32px – 38px
- **Target Desktop Body Text**: 18px (or larger where appropriate)
- **Target Mobile Body Text**: Must scale down to 14px – 15px with comfortable, clean line-height.
- **Proportional Scaling**: Scale down buttons, card headers, and secondary text on mobile views to keep the UI clean and uncluttered.

## Constraint 3: Strict Copywriting Rules (No Agentic Buzzwords)
- **Rule**: Under no circumstances use classic over-hyped marketing buzzwords.
- **Banned Words**:
  - elevating
  - built / built on / built upon (use "designed", "founded", "configured", etc.)
  - decades / decades of trust (use exact years or "multi-year", "long-term", etc.)
  - unrivaled
  - unparalleled
  - mastery (use "expertise", "focus", "insight", etc.)
  - transforming (use "shaping", "influencing", "evolving", etc.)
  - empowering
  - enriching
  - groundbreaking
  - premier (use "prime", "leading", "strategic", etc.)
- **Allowed Tone**: Direct, objective, transparent, and human-centric business language focusing purely on what the firm does and how it serves the client.
